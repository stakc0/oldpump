PIGGY BANK (ticker: PIGGY): The "piggy bank," or pig-shaped coin bank, has a history that spans several centuries. The term "pig" in Old English was "pygg," which referred to a type of clay used for making all kinds of household pottery in medieval England. People saved their money in jars and dishes made of pygg, known as "pygg jars." Over time and with changes in language, the pronunciation of "pygg" shifted closer to "pig," the English word for the animal. This prompted potters to start crafting savings banks in the shape of pigs, capitalizing on the wordplay. Pigs were also associated with prosperity and abundance in various cultures, complementing the idea of saving coins. Thus, the pig-shaped coin bank became a popular symbol for money conservation, especially among children. As the concept spread, piggy banks gained popularity around the world, becoming an iconic element of savings in many households.
